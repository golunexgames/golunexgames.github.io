Privacy Policy

Effective 11 November 2026 · Golunex Games

EnglishTürkçe

This policy explains what data the Wiserio app ("the game", on Android and iPhone/iPad) uses, why, and what you can do about it. Wiserio is made by Golunex Games ("we", "us"), an independent developer. You can reach us at golunexgames@gmail.com.

In short: you never give us your email, real name, contacts or precise location, and you do not need an account. To run the daily quiz, the leaderboards, leagues and Friend Rooms, the game creates an anonymous player ID. The free version shows ads through Google AdMob; optional purchases go through Google Play or the App Store. We use Google Firebase to count how the game is used and to receive crash reports. We do not sell your data.

1. Data on your phone

Your settings (language, sound, vibration, large text, reduce motion, colour-blind mode), your reminder time, some cached game state (so the home screen shows instantly) and the secret key of your anonymous player ID are saved on your device. Daily reminders are local notifications that your phone schedules by itself. Uninstalling the game or clearing its data deletes what is on the device.

2. Data our server processes

When you play, the game talks to our server. The server stores:

3. Services we use

  • Cloudflare (Cloudflare Workers and D1) hosts our server and database. Cloudflare Privacy Policy.
  • Google Firebase: Analytics counts how the game is used (for example that a daily quiz was started, a room created or the shop opened) with an app-instance ID, device model, OS version and approximate country, so we can improve the game; Crashlytics sends crash and error reports (device model, OS version, the error) so we can fix bugs; Cloud Messaging delivers push notifications. Firebase data is not linked to your name and is not used for ads. Firebase privacy.
  • Sign in with Google and Sign in with Apple, only if you choose them. Our server checks the sign-in and keeps only the account number described above. Google Privacy Policy · Apple Privacy Policy.
  • Google AdMob shows the ads in the free version (between rounds, and rewarded ads you choose to watch). Where the law requires it (for example in the EU, the UK and Switzerland) we first ask for your consent with Google's consent form, and on iPhone iOS asks whether apps may track you; you can change your choice at any time under Settings → Ad privacy choices (shown where it applies) or in your phone's settings. AdMob may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address and information about the ads shown to deliver, measure and, with your consent, personalise ads. Players with Wiserio Plus or "Remove ads" see no ads. How Google uses data from apps that use its services.
  • Google Play billing and the App Store handle purchases (Wiserio Plus, Remove ads, streak freezes). Google or Apple processes the payment; we never see your card or payment details. Our server checks each purchase with Google or Apple. Google Payments Privacy Notice · Apple Privacy Policy.

Your daily score can never be bought.

4. Legal basis

We process the data in section 2 to provide the features you use (performance of our agreement with you) and to keep the leaderboards fair, the questions accurate and the service secure (our legitimate interest). Usage statistics and crash reports rely on our legitimate interest in keeping the game working and improving it. Personalised ads rely on your consent where the law requires it.

5. How long we keep data

We keep your player record, name, avatar, country, results, seen questions and room plays for as long as your player ID exists, so your leaderboard history, streak and statistics remain available. We delete them when you delete your data (see section 6). Connection counters are deleted after two days. Question reports are kept, without any link to you, so that questions can still be checked. Firebase keeps analytics data for up to 14 months and crash reports for up to 90 days.

6. Your choices and rights

  • Delete your data in the game: Settings (the gear on the home screen) → Delete my data. This removes your player, name, results, room plays, purchases record, push token and Google or Apple link from our server (and revokes the Apple link with Apple), and resets the game on your phone. Your question reports are anonymised. A Plus subscription is not cancelled by this: cancel it in Google Play or in your Apple ID settings.
  • Change your display name, avatar or country: Settings.
  • Turn off reminders and notifications: Settings → Daily reminder, or in your phone's notification settings.
  • Ask for a copy, a correction or the deletion of your data by email: see Delete your data or write to golunexgames@gmail.com.

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in the EU or the KVKK in Turkey), you may also have the right to object to processing, to restrict it and to complain to your data protection authority.

7. Children

Wiserio is a general-audience quiz game and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has sent us personal data, email us and we will delete it.

8. Security

All traffic between the game and our server is encrypted (HTTPS). Player keys are stored only as hashes, and in the daily quiz the server keeps the right answers to itself and times and scores every answer.

9. Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date above, and for important changes we will also tell you in the game.

10. Contact

Golunex Games · golunexgames@gmail.com
