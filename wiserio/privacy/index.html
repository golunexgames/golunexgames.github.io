Privacy Policy

Effective 12 November 2026 · Golunex Games

EnglishTürkçe

This policy explains what data the Wiserio app ("the game", on Android and iPhone/iPad) uses, why, and what you can do about it. Wiserio is made by Golunex Games ("we", "us"), an independent developer. You can reach us at golunexgames@gmail.com.

Data controller: Murat Altınay (Golunex Games), Türkiye · golunexgames@gmail.com

In short: you never give us your email, real name, contacts or precise location, and you do not need an account. To run the daily quiz, the leaderboards, leagues and Friend Rooms, the game creates an anonymous player ID. The free version shows ads through Google AdMob; optional purchases go through Google Play or the App Store. On Android we use Google Firebase to count how the game is used and to receive crash reports. We do not sell your data.

1. Data on your phone

Your settings (language, sound, vibration, large text, reduce motion, colour-blind mode), your reminder time, some cached game state (so the home screen shows instantly) and the secret key of your anonymous player ID are saved on your device. Daily reminders are local notifications that your phone schedules by itself. Uninstalling the game or clearing its data deletes what is on the device.

3. Services we use

  • Cloudflare (Cloudflare Workers and D1) hosts our server and database. Cloudflare Privacy Policy.
  • Google Firebase: in the Android version only, Analytics counts how the game is used (for example that a daily quiz was started, a room created or the shop opened) with an app-instance ID, device model, OS version, approximate country and the game country you play in, so we can improve the game; Analytics starts only where no consent is needed or after you agree in the consent form; Crashlytics sends crash and error reports (device model, OS version, the error) so we can fix bugs; Cloud Messaging delivers push notifications on both Android and iPhone. The iPhone version does not include Firebase Analytics or Crashlytics. Firebase data is not linked to your name and is not used for ads. Firebase privacy.
  • Sign in with Google and Sign in with Apple, only if you choose them. Our server checks the sign-in and keeps only the account number described above. Google Privacy Policy · Apple Privacy Policy.
  • Google AdMob shows the ads in the free version (between rounds, and rewarded ads you choose to watch). Where the law requires it (for example in the EU, the UK and Switzerland) we first ask for your consent with Google's consent form, and on iPhone iOS asks whether apps may track you; you can change your choice at any time under Settings → Ad privacy choices (shown where it applies) or in your phone's settings. AdMob may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address and information about the ads shown to deliver, measure and, with your consent, personalise ads. When you watch a rewarded ad, we pass your anonymous player ID to AdMob so its confirmation reaches our server and the reward goes to the right player. Players with Wiserio Plus or "Remove ads" see no ads. How Google uses data from apps that use its services.
  • Google Play billing and the App Store handle purchases (Wiserio Plus, Remove ads, streak freezes). Google or Apple processes the payment; we never see your card or payment details. Our server checks each purchase with Google or Apple. Google Payments Privacy Notice · Apple Privacy Policy.

Your daily score can never be bought.

Transfers outside Türkiye and the EU: our server and database run on Cloudflare, and Firebase and AdMob are provided by Google. These providers may process data in the United States and other countries outside Türkiye and the European Union. These transfers rely on the providers' standard contractual clauses and other appropriate safeguards, as provided for in Article 9 of the KVKK and Chapter V of the GDPR.

4. Legal basis

We process the data in section 2 to provide the features you use (performance of our agreement with you) and to keep the leaderboards fair, the questions accurate and the service secure (our legitimate interest). Usage statistics and crash reports (Android only) rely on our legitimate interest in keeping the game working and improving it. Personalised ads rely on your consent where the law requires it.

5. How long we keep data

We keep your player record, name, avatar, country, results, seen questions and room plays for as long as your player ID exists, so your leaderboard history, streak and statistics remain available. We delete them when you delete your data (see section 6). Connection counters are deleted after two days. Question reports are kept, without any link to you, so that questions can still be checked.

Two kinds of records are kept, without any link to you, when you delete your data:

  • Purchase records (the fingerprint of the purchase token or transaction ID, the product, the purchase date and, for Plus, the end date). The player ID in them is replaced by a random value. We keep them so the same purchase cannot be redeemed twice, and so Plus or Remove ads can be restored later to a new player from the same Google Play or App Store account. They have no fixed deletion date, because a purchase can be restored at any time while the game is offered.
  • Rooms you set up (the room code, the optional room name, the list of questions and the country), so friends who played them still see their results. The owner is replaced by a random value. Rooms close to new plays 48 hours after they are created; the room record itself currently has no automatic deletion date. If a room name contains something personal, email us and we will remove it.

Firebase keeps analytics data for up to 14 months and crash reports for up to 90 days.

6. Your choices and rights

  • Delete your data in the game: Settings (the gear on the home screen) → Delete my data. This removes your player, name, results, room plays, invite data, push token and Google or Apple link from our server (and revokes the Apple link with Apple), and resets the game on your phone. Your question reports, your purchase records and the rooms you set up are kept without any link to you (see section 5). A Plus subscription is not cancelled by this: cancel it in Google Play or in your Apple ID settings.
  • Change your display name, avatar or country: Settings.
  • Turn off reminders and notifications: Settings → Daily reminder turns off the daily reminder; to stop all notifications (also "a friend beat your score" and room results), turn notifications off for Wiserio in your phone's settings.
  • Ask for a copy, a correction or the deletion of your data by email: see Delete your data or write to golunexgames@gmail.com.

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in the EU or the KVKK in Türkiye), you may also have the right to object to processing, to restrict it and to complain to your data protection authority.

US residents (for example under the CCPA in California): we do not sell your personal information. When Google AdMob shows personalised ads, this may count as "sharing" personal information for cross-context behavioural advertising under the CCPA. You can turn this off at any time: on iPhone, do not allow tracking (Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking); on Android, delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads, or Settings → Google → Ads, depending on the phone). Where it is shown, you can also use Settings → Ad privacy choices in the game. You also have the right to know, correct and delete your personal information, and we will not treat you differently for using these rights.

7. Children

Wiserio is a general-audience quiz game and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has sent us personal data, email us and we will delete it.

8. Security

All traffic between the game and our server is encrypted (HTTPS). Player keys are stored only as hashes, and in the daily quiz the server keeps the right answers to itself and times and scores every answer.

9. Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date above, and for important changes we will also tell you in the game.

10. Contact
