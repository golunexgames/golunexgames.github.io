What's inside

More than a daily set

Your country's own questions

Every launch country has its own pool, written from the inside: food, slang, places, sport, the things you'd know if you grew up there.

Leaderboards and streaks

Daily country and world rankings, a weekly view that adds up your last seven days, and a streak you can protect with a freeze.

Friend rooms

Create a room, send the code or link. Up to ten friends play the same questions in their own time within 48 hours, results side by side.

Free play, no pressure

Want more? Free Play gives you fresh questions with no ranking, and it remembers what you've already seen.

Checked twice

Every question needs two independent sources and an independent check before it goes live. Spot a mistake anyway? Report it in one tap.
