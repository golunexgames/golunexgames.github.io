Our games

Two games, two moods

One is quiet and glowing, played in loops. The other is bright and quick, played against the whole country. Both fit in a coffee break.

Twicetold

A time-loop puzzle

Every loop rewinds time and leaves an echo that repeats your earlier moves. Send one self to hold a plate, walk through the door it opens, and never step where an echo has to go. Ninety hand-checked levels, a daily puzzle, and hints that never spoil the fun.

Wiserio

Daily trivia: the world and your country

Ten fresh questions every day: six about the world, four about your own country. Everyone in your country gets the same set, so you can see where you stand against friends, family and the whole nation. Twenty seconds a question, a "did you know?" after each one, and a streak worth keeping.

How we work

Not one genre. One habit.

Puzzles today, trivia tomorrow, something else after that. Whatever the genre, every Golunex game is built on the same four promises.

Daily by design

A daily puzzle, a daily quiz, a streak to protect. Small rituals that give you a reason to come back, not a reason to grind.

Made for the whole world

Nine languages from day one. In Wiserio, every country gets its own questions, written from the inside, not from a tourist brochure.

Checked by hand

A solver plays every puzzle level before it ships. Every trivia question needs two independent sources and passes a separate check before you ever see it.

Fair play

Hints you choose, scores you earn. Nothing that matters is for sale, and nothing interrupts you mid-move or mid-question.

The studio

A one-person studio from Turkey

Golunex Games started in 2026 with a puzzle and kept going with a quiz. Every game is designed, built and tested by one person, with care for the details players feel: how a move sounds, how a level teaches, how a question surprises.

The crescent G in our logo is a moon over a quiet night of play, with a controller tucked inside. "Lune" is right there in the name.
