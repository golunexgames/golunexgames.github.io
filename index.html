The studio

A one-person studio from Turkey

Golunex Games is a small independent studio. Every game is designed, built and tested by hand, with care for the details players feel: how a move sounds, how a level teaches, how a hint helps.

The crescent G in our logo is a moon over a quiet night of play, with a controller tucked inside.
