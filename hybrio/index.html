What's inside

Small experiments, endless variety

Breed and discover

Every plant carries genes for leaf shape, arrangement, pattern, colour and flower. Crossing mixes them, and now and then a gene mutates into something rare. You see the chance of a new species before you cross.

6,720 species

Each with its own Latin name and a rarity from one to five stars. Complete genus sets by finding all eight colours of a genus.

Shiny plants

Once in a while a cross grows into a shiny plant with a golden glow, worth five times as much and marked in gold in your album.

Orders and the wanted species

Customers ask for plants with certain traits. Every week there is also a wanted species with a big reward for the first one you grow.

Upgrade your greenhouse

A grow lamp, a beehive for more mutations, a market stall for better prices and a bird feeder for wild seeds. Four backdrops, from a glass conservatory to a starry night.

Cozy and relaxed

Plants keep growing while you are away, and a notification tells you when they are ready. Claim a daily reward and keep your streak going.

Offline, no account

No sign-in. Ads only when you choose to watch one for a reward, and coin packs are optional.
