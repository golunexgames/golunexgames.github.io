Privacy Policy

Effective 9 November 2026 · Golunex Games

EnglishTürkçe

This policy explains what data the Wisewhere app ("the game") uses, why, and what you can do about it. Wisewhere is made by Golunex Games ("we", "us"). You can reach us at golunexgames@gmail.com.

In short: you never give us your email, real name, contacts or location, and you do not need an account. To run the daily quiz, the leaderboards and Friend Rooms, the game creates an anonymous player ID. Wisewhere has no ads, no analytics, no tracking and no purchases. We do not sell your data.

1. Data on your phone

Your settings (language, sound, vibration, large text), your daily reminder time and the secret key of your anonymous player ID are saved on your device. Daily reminders are local notifications that your phone schedules by itself; nothing is sent to a push notification server. Uninstalling the game or clearing its data deletes what is on the device.

2. Data our server processes

When you play, the game talks to our server. The server stores:

DataWhy
A random player ID and a secret key, created automatically on first launch (the key is stored only as a one-way hash)To recognise your game without an account
Your display name. It starts as "Player-" plus six characters, and you can change it in the gameShown publicly on the leaderboards and to the other players in your Friend Rooms
Your country: taken from your phone's region setting, or, if there is none, from Cloudflare's guess based on your connection, or as you pick it. You can change it once every 30 daysTo choose your local questions and your country leaderboard. We never ask for your location
Daily quiz answers: for each question, the answer you chose, the time you took and the points; plus your score, date and country for the dayTo score and rank results fairly, show your streak and your own statistics
Which questions you have already seenSo Free Play does not repeat questions
Friend Room plays: the room code, your answers, time, points and score. Rooms close to new plays after 48 hoursTo show everyone's results side by side
Question reports: the question, the reason you picked, an optional short note and whether you had answered it correctlyTo check and fix questions. If you delete your data, your reports stay but are no longer linked to you
If you choose "Continue with Google": the stable account number Google gives our app for your account (not your email, name or photo)So you can keep your name, results and streak on a new phone. Signing in is optional

Like every website and app server, our hosting provider receives your IP address and basic request information (such as time and app version) to deliver the request and protect the service from abuse. We do not store IP addresses in our game database.

3. Services we use

  • Cloudflare (Cloudflare Workers and D1) hosts our server and database. Cloudflare Privacy Policy.
  • Sign in with Google, only if you choose it. Our server checks the sign-in with Google and keeps only the account number described above. Google Privacy Policy.

Wisewhere shows no ads, offers no purchases, and uses no analytics or tracking tools.

4. Legal basis

We process the data in section 2 to provide the features you use (performance of our agreement with you) and to keep the leaderboards fair, the questions accurate and the service secure (our legitimate interest).

5. How long we keep data

We keep your player record, name, country, results, seen questions and room plays for as long as your player ID exists, so your leaderboard history, streak and statistics remain available. We delete them when you delete your data (see section 6). Question reports are kept, without any link to you, so that questions can still be checked.

6. Your choices and rights

  • Delete your data in the game: Settings (the gear on the home screen) → Delete my data. This removes your player, name, results, room plays and Google link from our server and resets the game on your phone. Your question reports are anonymised.
  • Change your display name or country: Settings → Name or Country.
  • Disconnect Google: write to us at golunexgames@gmail.com, or use Delete my data (which removes the link along with everything else).
  • Turn off daily reminders: Settings → Daily reminder, or in your phone's notification settings.
  • Ask for a copy, a correction or the deletion of your data by email: see Delete your data or write to golunexgames@gmail.com.

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in the EU or the KVKK in Turkey), you may also have the right to object to processing, to restrict it and to complain to your data protection authority.

7. Children

Wisewhere is a general-audience quiz game and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has sent us personal data, email us and we will delete it.

8. Security

All traffic between the game and our server is encrypted (HTTPS). Player keys are stored only as hashes, and in the daily quiz the server keeps the right answers to itself and times and scores every answer.

9. Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date above, and for important changes we will also tell you in the game.

10. Contact

Golunex Games · golunexgames@gmail.com
