Privacy Policy

Effective 6 November 2026 · Updated 6 November 2026 (Sign in with Google) · Golunex Games

EnglishTürkçe

This policy explains what data the Twicetold app ("the game") uses, why, and what you can do about it. Twicetold is made by Golunex Games ("we", "us"). You can reach us at golunexgames@gmail.com.

In short: you never give us your email, real name, contacts or location. Signing in with Google is optional and only needed to buy hints. To run the daily puzzle, the leaderboard and your hint balance, the game creates an anonymous player ID. We do not sell your data.

1. Data that stays on your phone

Your level progress, stars and best scores, settings (language, sound, music), which tutorials you have seen, your daily streak and the secret key of your anonymous player ID are saved on your device only. Uninstalling the game or clearing its data deletes them.

2. Data our server processes

When you use an online feature (daily puzzle, leaderboard, hints, shop), the game talks to our server. The server stores:

DataWhy
A random player ID and a secret key, created automatically on first use (the key is stored only as a one-way hash)To recognise your game without an account
Your display name. It starts as "Player-" plus four characters, and you can change it in the gameShown publicly on the leaderboards
Daily puzzle results: date, the moves you played, number of moves, time from opening the puzzle to solving it, points, stars, whether a hint was usedTo score and rank results fairly and to reject impossible or automated results
Hint balance and the hints you unlocked (level, type, time)So a hint you paid for stays unlocked and is never charged twice
If you choose "Continue with Google": the stable account number Google gives our app for your Google account (not your email, name or photo)To keep bought hints, your name and results with you on a new phone. Signing in is only required to buy hints
Purchases, once the shop is open: product, the purchase token from Google Play, timeTo credit each purchase exactly once. We never receive your payment details

Like every website and app server, our hosting provider receives your IP address and basic request information (such as time and app version) to deliver the request and protect the service from abuse. We do not store IP addresses in our game database.

3. Services we use

  • Cloudflare (Cloudflare Workers and D1) hosts our server and database. Cloudflare Privacy Policy.
  • Sign in with Google (Android Credential Manager), only if you choose it. Google shows its own account picker; our server checks the sign-in with Google and keeps only the account number described above.
  • Google Play billing, once in-app purchases are available, handles payments. Google Privacy Policy.
  • Google Play Integrity, when enabled, lets our server check that a result was sent by a genuine copy of the game. We receive a verdict about the app and device, not your personal data.
  • Google AdMob, when rewarded ads are available, shows an ad only when you choose to watch one for a hint. AdMob may collect device identifiers (such as the advertising ID), IP address and ad interactions, as described in how Google uses data from apps that use its services. Where the law requires it (for example in the EEA and the UK), the game asks for your consent first, and you can change your choice later in your device or in-game settings.

We do not use analytics or tracking tools in Twicetold.

4. Legal basis

We process the data in section 2 to provide the features you use (performance of our agreement with you) and to keep the leaderboard fair and the service secure (our legitimate interest). Personalised ads, where offered, rely on your consent.

5. How long we keep data

We keep your player record, name, results and hint records for as long as your player ID exists, so your leaderboard history and hints remain available. We delete them when you ask us to (see section 6). Purchase records may be kept for as long as tax or consumer law requires.

6. Your choices and rights

  • Change your display name in the game: Daily Puzzle → Leaderboard → the pencil button.
  • Play offline: the campaign works without an internet connection, and nothing is sent to our server unless you use an online feature.
  • Ask for a copy, a correction or the deletion of your data: follow Delete your data or email golunexgames@gmail.com.

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in the EU or the KVKK in Türkiye), you may also have the right to object to processing, to restrict it and to complain to your data protection authority.

7. Children

Twicetold is a general-audience puzzle game and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has sent us personal data, email us and we will delete it.

8. Security

All traffic between the game and our server is encrypted (HTTPS). Player keys are stored only as hashes, and the server checks every daily result by replaying its moves before accepting it.

9. Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date above, and for important changes we will also tell you in the game.

10. Contact

Golunex Games · golunexgames@gmail.com
