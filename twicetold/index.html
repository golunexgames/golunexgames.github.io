A time-loop puzzle

Twicetold: solve it together with your past.

When a loop ends, time rewinds and your earlier moves play again as an echo. Send one self to hold a plate, walk through the door it opens, and never step where an echo needs to be: one collision is a paradox.

How to play

Three gestures, endless loops

Swipe to moveEvery move is one step. Reach the exit before the loop runs out of steps.
Tap to waitWaiting is a move too: let a door open, or let your echo pass first.
Hold to rewindEnd the loop. Time goes back, and your past self replays every move you made.
